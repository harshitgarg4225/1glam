WhatsApp message templates (advanced, optional)

You don't need any of this to get started. WhatsApp only lets businesses send automated messages using pre-approved templates (created in Meta Business Manager). If you add the matching template names below, your quotes, reminders and review requests will send reliably at any time. Leave them blank and 1Glam sends normal text instead (which WhatsApp only delivers within 24 hours of the client's last message). The one-tap Notify team and WhatsApp buttons always work with no setup.

Template variables — Booking request: {{1}} client, {{2}} occasion, {{3}} date · Approval: {{1}} client, {{2}} date, {{3}} price · Team alert: {{1}} specialist, {{2}} occasion, {{3}} date, {{4}} venue · Quote/Invoice/Contract: {{1}} client, {{2}} occasion, {{3}} date, {{4}} link · Payment reminder: {{1}} client, {{2}} advance/balance, {{3}} amount, {{4}} date · Pre-event reminder: {{1}} client, {{2}} date, {{3}} time · Review: {{1}} client, {{2}} business, {{3}} review link.

Service Descriptions & Add-ons

Add a description and optional add-ons to each service shown on your public booking page. Add-ons format: Name:Price comma-separated, e.g. Hair Styling:2000, False Lashes:500. Leave blank to hide.

Sheet Protection

Enabling protection applies a read-only lock to your Google Sheet in Drive — it shows a padlock icon and prevents accidental edits or moves by anyone with access. The file owner can still remove the restriction from Drive directly.

If your sheet was deleted, use Recover Sheet to re-link the best matching sheet in your Drive, or create a fresh one if none is found.
