What BusyDays does

BusyDays is booking and client-communication software for independent appointment-based professionals — makeup artists, salons, photographers, clinics, coaches and studios. It gives you a shareable booking page, a calendar that stays in sync, and one tidy inbox for every enquiry, payment and message, so nothing slips through the cracks.

How it works

1

Connect your Google account

Sign in with Google to create your workspace. BusyDays sets up a private Google Sheet you own to store your leads and bookings.

2

Share your booking page

Send clients your link from Instagram or WhatsApp. Enquiries and bookings land in your dashboard automatically.

3

Stay in sync everywhere

Confirmed bookings appear on your Google Calendar, documents are generated for you, and reminders go out on time — hands-free.

How BusyDays uses your Google data

With your explicit permission, BusyDays uses Google Sheets to store your leads and bookings in a spreadsheet you own, Google Calendar to create and read your booking events, and Drive file access limited to the files this app creates. Your data lives in your own Google account, is isolated to your workspace, and is never sold or shared with other users. You can disconnect or delete it at any time. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
